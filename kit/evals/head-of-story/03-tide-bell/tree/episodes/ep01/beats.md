# Beats

## B1 introduction
duration: 8

Fog. Mira watches the empty sea from the flats; her father's boat is late.

## B2 development
duration: 10

She wades out at low tide to the drowned church tower.

## B3 twist
duration: 10

The bell's rope is rotten; she rings it with a stone instead.

## B4 conclusion
duration: 12

A horn answers, and Abel's boat noses out of the fog.
