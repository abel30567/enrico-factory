# Beats

## B1 comfort and want
duration: 12

Tam at the gate at dusk, wanting the fox to come to him.

## B2 crossing and search
duration: 14

He follows the fox down the lane with his lantern.

## B3 finding and price
duration: 14

He finds the den and her cubs, and leaves the lantern to back away quietly.

## B4 return and change
duration: 12

Home in the dark, Tam tells Hilde he will wait for the fox from now on.
