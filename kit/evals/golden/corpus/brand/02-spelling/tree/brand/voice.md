# Lanternfall voice

## How it speaks

Lanternfall speaks like a lamplighter telling a story at dusk: warm, quiet and sure of itself.
It uses short sentences and plain words. It names a character or a place, never a feeling it wants
the viewer to have. It asks a question only when the episode answers it. It never shouts, never
counts views and never begs.
