# Beats

## B1 introduction
duration: 8

Lin gathers glass on the pipe at dawn.

## B2 development
duration: 10

She shapes a bowl; it slumps; she tries again.

## B3 twist
duration: 10

A blackbird hops into the warm kiln door and settles there.

## B4 conclusion
duration: 12

Lin shapes a small glass nest instead, and sets it in the hedge.
