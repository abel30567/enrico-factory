# Script

## S1 The kiln
beats: B1

Dawn. Lin dips the pipe into the furnace and turns out a gather.

L1 LIN: First gather.

## S2 The bowl
beats: B2, B3

The bowl slumps. Lin starts again. A blackbird hops into the warm kiln door and fluffs itself up in the heat.

L2 LIN: You too?

## S3 The hedge
beats: B4

Lin turns a fresh gather into a small glass nest, lets it cool, and sets it in the hedge by the door.

L3 LIN: There. Warm enough.
