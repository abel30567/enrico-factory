# Script

## S1 The porch
beats: B1

Pim drags the big kite out of the shed.

L1 PIM: Let me take the big one.
L2 GRAN: Then take the gloves. The line bites when the wind turns.
L3 PIM: I won't need them.

Gran pushes the gloves into Pim's coat pocket anyway.

## S2 The hill
beats: B2, B3

The wind rises. Pim hauls the big kite up the hill, leaning into the gusts, and launches it at the top. The kite climbs. Then the wind turns, and the line bites a red cut across her bare palm. She holds on, and pulls the gloves from her pocket.

L4 PIM: Gloves.

## S3 The porch again
beats: B4

Pim hangs the gloves on the kite's hook.

L5 GRAN: Yours now.
