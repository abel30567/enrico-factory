# Script

## S1 The shop
beats: B1

Evening. Rin polishes the old hand lamp at the bench.

L1 RIN: Let me do the street tonight.
L2 MASTER: Ha! Off you go then, my little firefly, and mind you don't set the whole town alight!
L3 RIN: I won't.

## S2 The street
beats: B2, B3

The street lights die all at once. Rin takes the old hand lamp out into the dark street and lights lamp after lamp by hand. On the last, the flame catches her palm.

L4 RIN: Last one.

## S3 The shop again
beats: B4

Rin hangs the hand lamp on the master's hook.

L5 MASTER: Your hook now.
