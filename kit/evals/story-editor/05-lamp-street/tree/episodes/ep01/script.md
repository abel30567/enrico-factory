# Script

## S1 The shop
beats: B1

Evening. Rin polishes the old hand lamp at the bench.

L1 RIN: Let me do the street tonight.
L2 MASTER: Take the spare key to the lamp tower. If the power fails, the tower switch is the only way.
L3 RIN: I won't need it.

## S2 The street
beats: B2, B3

The street lights die. Rin lights lamp after lamp by hand. On the last, the flame catches her palm.

L4 RIN: Last one.

## S3 The shop again
beats: B4

Rin hangs the hand lamp on the master's hook.

L5 MASTER: Your hook now.
