# Beats

## B1 introduction
duration: 10

Aya climbs the tower for the evening practice.

## B2 development
duration: 10

She rings the scale; one bell sounds dull.

## B3 twist
duration: 10

Inside the dull bell: a pigeon sitting on its nest.

## B4 conclusion
duration: 10

Aya rings the scale with one bell left out, and the gap sounds like a tune.
