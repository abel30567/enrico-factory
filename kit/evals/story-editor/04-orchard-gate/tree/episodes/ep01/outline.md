# Outline

## S1 Open gate
beats: B1

Dawn: the gate is open and the apples are gone.

## S2 The watch
beats: B2

Bel watches all night; at dawn the gate is open anyway, and Ned laughs.

## S3 The traps
beats: B3

Bel follows hoof marks to the wall, finds the deer path under it, and mends the gate with Ned.

## S4 The mended wall
beats: B4

The owner thanks Bel and the orchard is safe.

## S5 The fawn
beats: B5

A fawn watches through the new gate.
