# Beats

## B1 teaser
duration: 60

The gate stands open at dawn; apples are missing.

## B2 act 1
duration: 300

Bel sets a watch; her brother Ned scoffs; the next dawn the gate is open again although she never slept.

## B3 act 2
duration: 300

Bel's traps fail and catch Ned instead; the owner threatens to sell; her lowest point is the sale notice nailed to the gate.

## B4 act 3
duration: 300

Bel finds the deer path under the wall and mends the gate, with Ned's help.

## B5 tag
duration: 60

A fawn watches through the new gate.
