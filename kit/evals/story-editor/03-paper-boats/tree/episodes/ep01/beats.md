# Beats

## B1 introduction
duration: 8

The rain stops; Oti and Sem fold paper boats on the step.

## B2 development
duration: 12

They race the boats down the gutter, past the bakery, to the drain.

## B3 twist
duration: 10

Oti unfolds a soaked boat: it is a page of their grandmother's letters.

## B4 conclusion
duration: 10

They dry the pages on the warm oven door, boats and letters both kept.
