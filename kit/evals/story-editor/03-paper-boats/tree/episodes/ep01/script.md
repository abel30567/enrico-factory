# Script

## S1 The step
beats: B1

The rain stops. Oti and Sem fold paper boats on the front step.

L1 OTI: Mine's faster.
L2 SEM: Mine's folded better.

## S2 The gutter
beats: B2, B3

The boats race down the gutter past the bakery. Sem's boat reaches the drain first.

L3 SEM: Mine wins.
L4 OTI: Again, then.

## S3 The oven
beats: B4

In the bakery, they lay the wet pages on the warm oven door.

L5 OTI: Gran's writing.
L6 SEM: We'll keep them.
