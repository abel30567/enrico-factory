# Lanternfall charter

Lanternfall is a quiet animated serial for children from eight and their families. Wick, the lamplighter's apprentice, keeps the Low Lanes lit while the lamps go out one street at a time.

## Tone

Warm, quiet and sure of itself. Wonder, never fright.

## Limits

- Wick never carries a weapon and never hurts anyone.
- No one is mocked for being afraid of the dark.
- The show never tells who puts the lamps out before the episode does.
