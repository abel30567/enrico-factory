# The tide bell: charter

The owner approved this charter.

C1. The show follows the keeper of the tide bell on a small harbour island.
C2. Each episode is one tide, from low water to the next low water.
C3. The bell rings only when the sea road floods.
C4. No one on the island harms an animal.
C5. Every episode ends with the keeper writing in the tide book.

The owner did not decide how many episodes there are or who the keeper's family are.
