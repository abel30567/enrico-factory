# Script

## S1 The yard
beats: B1

Ysa strokes the old horse's nose.

L1 YSA: Please, just once.

## S2 The reins
beats: B2, B3

Her uncle shakes his head. Ysa tells him the horse only obeys a singer, which is not true, and sings to it. He hands her the reins.

L2 YSA: Watch me.

## S3 The parade
beats: B4

Ysa rides at the front of the parade.

L3 YSA: Look at us!
