# Beats

## B1 introduction
duration: 10

Ysa wants to ride the old plough horse in the hill farm's harvest parade.

## B2 development
duration: 10

Her uncle says she is too small to hold the reins.

## B3 twist
duration: 10

Ysa shows him she can lead the horse round the yard on foot, and he gives her the reins.

## B4 conclusion
duration: 10

Ysa rides the plough horse at the front of the harvest parade.
