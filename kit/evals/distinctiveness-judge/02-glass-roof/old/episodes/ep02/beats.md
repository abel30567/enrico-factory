# Beats

## B1 introduction
duration: 10

The storm hits while the last pane of the glass roof is still missing; rain pours onto the orchids.

## B2 development
duration: 10

Wren climbs the roof in the wind with the pane on her back.

## B3 twist
duration: 10

The pane is too big; she fits it at an angle, and it becomes a rain spout.

## B4 conclusion
duration: 10

The rain runs off the spout into the water butt, and the orchids stay dry.
