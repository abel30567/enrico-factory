# Beats

## B1 introduction
duration: 10

The last pane of the glass roof is gone, the morning the storm is due.

## B2 development
duration: 10

Wren searches every frame and corner for it.

## B3 twist
duration: 10

A magpie has carried the pane's putty off to its nest.

## B4 conclusion
duration: 10

Wren leaves the magpie a button for the putty and fits the pane as the rain starts.
