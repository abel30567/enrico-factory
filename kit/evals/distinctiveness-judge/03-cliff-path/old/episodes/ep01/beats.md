# Beats

## B1 introduction
duration: 10

Dov climbs the cliff path to bring his grandfather's lunch to the lighthouse.

## B2 development
duration: 10

Halfway up, the path has crumbled away.

## B3 twist
duration: 10

In the cliff face there is a door; through it, the path is whole again, as it was yesterday.

## B4 conclusion
duration: 10

Dov reaches the lighthouse with the lunch still warm.
