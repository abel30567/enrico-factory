# Beats

## B1 introduction
duration: 10

Lio dives into the kelp cove with her net, looking for a lost ring.

## B2 development
duration: 10

Deep in the kelp, something keeps tugging at her net.

## B3 twist
duration: 10

The tugging is an otter holding the lost ring, and it will not let go.

## B4 conclusion
duration: 10

Lio trades the otter a shell for the ring, surfaces and waves goodbye.
