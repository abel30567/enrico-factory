# Beats

## B1 you
duration: 30

Ros mends nets in the loft above the harbour, alone since her brother left.

## B2 need
duration: 30

The winter catch fails; the harbour needs the big net mended by the new moon.

## B3 go
duration: 40

Ros climbs to the old loft where her brother kept his tools.

## B4 search
duration: 40

She searches the loft for his netting needle among the gear.

## B5 find
duration: 30

She finds the needle, and a letter he left in its case.

## B6 take
duration: 30

Ros reads the letter and works through the night on the big net.

## B7 return
duration: 30

At the new moon the boats go out with the mended net.

## B8 change
duration: 30

Ros writes back to her brother, for the first time.
