# Beats

## B1 comfort and want
duration: 12

Bo sets up her stall on the pier.

## B2 crossing and search
duration: 14

A gull steals a fish; Bo chases it along the pier.

## B3 finding and price
duration: 14

Back at the stall, gulls have the rest; Bo shoos them and loses her cap.

## B4 return and change
duration: 12

She sells the last fish to an old man wearing her cap.
