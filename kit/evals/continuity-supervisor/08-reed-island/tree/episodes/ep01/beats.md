# Beats

duration: 12

## B2 crossing and search
duration: 14

She poles the punt across as the light goes.

## B3 finding and price
duration: 14

On the island at night she waits in the reeds and the punt drifts off.

## B4 return and change
duration: 12

Dawn: the herons wake her, and she wades home.
