# Beats

## B1 comfort and want
duration: 12

Night on the farm; Lark wants to find the lost goat before morning.

## B2 crossing and search
duration: 14

She follows the bell up the goat track by moonlight.

## B3 finding and price
duration: 14

She finds the goat on a ledge and tears her hand pulling it up.

## B4 return and change
duration: 12

Back at the pen at first light, she ties the bell tighter.
