# Beats

## B1 comfort and want
duration: 12

Edda in the boathouse at night, wanting to sail the old boat.

## B2 crossing and search
duration: 14

She carries a lantern down the slipway to find the launching chocks.

## B3 finding and price
duration: 14

She knocks out the chocks; the boat slides and pins her coat.

## B4 return and change
duration: 12

Cal frees her; they watch the boat float.
