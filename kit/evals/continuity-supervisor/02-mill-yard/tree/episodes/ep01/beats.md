# Beats

## B1 comfort and want
duration: 12

Joss wants to run the mill alone for a day.

## B2 crossing and search
duration: 14

She carries the first sack across the yard to the stone.

## B3 finding and price
duration: 14

She opens the sluice; the wheel turns and the race soaks her.

## B4 return and change
duration: 12

Wim finds her drenched and grinning, and the flour runs.
