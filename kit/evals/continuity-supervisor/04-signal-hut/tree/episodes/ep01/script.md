# Script

## S1 The hut
beats: B1

Vey brews tea in the signal hut.

L1 VEY: Quiet night.

## S2 The ladder
beats: B2

A flag on the headland. Vey climbs the ladder.

L2 VEY: Storm.

## S3 The mast
beats: B3

Vey flashes the lamp through the rain until its glass cracks.

L3 VEY: Answer me.

## S4 Dawn
beats: B4

A light answers from the south. Vey mends the lamp.

L4 VEY: Heard.
