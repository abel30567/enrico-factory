# Beats

## B1 introduction
duration: 10

Bex opens her stall at the night market; will her best lantern light tonight?

## B2 development
duration: 10

She changes the wick and the oil, and it still will not light.

## B3 twist
duration: 10

A moth's cocoon is spun over the lantern's vent.

## B4 conclusion
duration: 10

A stranger leaves a sealed letter with Bex's name on it, and the market goes dark.
