# Script

## S1 The stall
beats: B1

Bex hangs her lanterns. The best one stays dark.

L1 BEX: Light, please.

## S2 The wick
beats: B2, B3

Bex changes the wick and the oil. Nothing. She lifts the cap: a cocoon over the vent.

L2 BEX: Oh, little sleeper.

## S3 The light
beats: B4

Bex sets the cocoon on a leaf. The lantern blazes, and the crowd turns to look.

L3 BEX: Well. Look at that.
