# Beats

## B1 introduction
duration: 10

Mela poles her flat boat into the reed marsh to find the heron that sings at dusk.

## B2 development
duration: 10

The reeds close behind her, and she cannot see the way back.

## B3 twist
duration: 10

The heron lands on her bow and starts to sing.

## B4 conclusion
duration: 10

Mela follows the heron's flight home, singing its tune back to it.
