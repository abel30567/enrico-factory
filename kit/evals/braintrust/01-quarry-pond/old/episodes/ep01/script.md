# Script

## S1 The launch
beats: B1

Tavi sets her paper boat on the water and blows it off.

L1 TAVI: Go on, little boat.

## S2 The reeds
beats: B2, B3

The boat sticks in the reeds. Tavi sits on the bank and waits until a gust shakes it free.

L2 TAVI: Come on. Come on.

## S3 The rock
beats: B4

The boat bumps the far rock. Tavi punches the air.

L3 TAVI: First to the rock!
