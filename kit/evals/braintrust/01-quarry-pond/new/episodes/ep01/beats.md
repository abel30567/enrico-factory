# Beats

## B1 introduction
duration: 10

Tavi launches her paper boat across the quarry pond, racing it to the far rock.

## B2 development
duration: 10

The boat drifts into the reeds and sticks there.

## B3 twist
duration: 10

Tavi kicks off her shoes, wades in to her knees and parts the reeds with a stick.

## B4 conclusion
duration: 10

The boat bumps the far rock first, and Tavi cheers.
