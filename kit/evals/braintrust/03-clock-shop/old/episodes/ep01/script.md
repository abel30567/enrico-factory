# Script

## S1 Closing
beats: B1

Ivo turns the sign. Behind him the tall clock stops at seven.

L1 IVO: Seven again?

## S2 The case
beats: B2, B3

Ivo winds the small clocks one by one. The tall one stays silent. He takes his own key from his pocket and opens its case: a cricket.

L2 IVO: Who's in there?

## S3 The apple
beats: B4

Ivo sets down a slice of apple. A cricket climbs out, and the tall clock ticks.

L3 IVO: Off you go, timekeeper.
