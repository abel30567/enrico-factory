# Beats

## B1 introduction
duration: 10

At closing, the tall clock in Ivo's shop stops at seven.

## B2 development
duration: 10

Ivo winds every small clock; the tall one stays silent.

## B3 twist
duration: 10

Ivo presses his ear to the locked case and hears a cricket chirping in the works.

## B4 conclusion
duration: 10

Ivo sets a slice of apple by the case; the cricket climbs out and the clock ticks again.
