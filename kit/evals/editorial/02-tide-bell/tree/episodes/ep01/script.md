# Script

## S1 The flats
beats: B1, B2

Fog on the flats. Mira kicks off her boots and wades towards the tower.

L1 MIRA: Late again.

## S2 The tower
beats: B3, B4

The rope falls apart in her hand. She strikes the bell with a stone. A horn answers; Abel's boat comes out of the fog.

L2 MIRA: Hear it.
L3 ABEL: Mira!
