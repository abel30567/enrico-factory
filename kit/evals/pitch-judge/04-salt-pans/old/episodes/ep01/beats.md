# Beats

## B1 introduction
duration: 8

Yara rakes salt into heaps as dark clouds come in.

## B2 development
duration: 10

The first drops fall; she cannot cover every heap.

## B3 twist
duration: 10

Tomas tips the rake handle-down to cut a channel, and the water runs off.

## B4 conclusion
duration: 12

The heaps stand dry; Yara cuts the next channel herself.
