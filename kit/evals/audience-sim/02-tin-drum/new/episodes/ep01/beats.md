# Beats

## B1 introduction
duration: 10

Pim polishes his tin drum for the village parade.

## B2 development
duration: 10

At the parade, the drum's skin splits on the first beat.

## B3 twist
duration: 10

Pim blows the brass whistle in the drum's rhythm, and the band follows him.

## B4 conclusion
duration: 10

The band marches the whole street in time behind Pim.
