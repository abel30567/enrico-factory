# Beats

## B1 introduction
duration: 10

Kesh waits at the bus stop as the snow falls; the bus comes and she gets on.

## B2 development
duration: 10

Halfway home, the bus slides into a drift; the engine will not start and the radio is dead.

## B3 twist
duration: 10

Kesh finds the driver's old sledge under the back seat.

## B4 conclusion
duration: 10

Kesh and the driver sledge the last mile, and her mother's porch light is on.
