# Beats

## B1 introduction
duration: 10

Halfway home, the school bus slides into a snowdrift with Kesh the last child aboard.

## B2 development
duration: 10

The engine will not start and the radio is dead; snow climbs the doors.

## B3 twist
duration: 10

Kesh finds the driver's old sledge under the back seat.

## B4 conclusion
duration: 10

Kesh and the driver sledge the last mile, and her mother's porch light is on.
