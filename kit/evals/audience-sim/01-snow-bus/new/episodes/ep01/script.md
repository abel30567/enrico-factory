# Script

## S1 The drift
beats: B1

The bus slides off the road into a drift. Kesh grips the seat.

L1 KESH: It's so cold.

## S2 The sledge
beats: B2, B3

The driver turns the key; the engine dies. Kesh crawls under the back seat and drags out a sledge.

L2 KESH: A sledge!

## S3 The porch
beats: B4

Kesh and the driver sledge down the hill toward a porch light.

L3 KESH: Faster!
