# Beats

## B1 introduction
duration: 10

Nuri drops a leaf boat into the mill race and runs beside it along the bank.

## B2 development
duration: 10

The water speeds toward the mill wheel, and Nuri has to outrun it to the sluice.

## B3 twist
duration: 10

At the sluice, Nuri hauls the lever, and the water turns away from the wheel into the duck pond.

## B4 conclusion
duration: 10

The leaf boat sails out among the ducks, and Nuri bows.
