# Script

## S1 The count
beats: B1

Zeb counts the figs. Three are gone.

L1 ZEB: Three gone again.

## S2 Dawn
beats: B2, B3

Zeb crouches behind the trough. At dawn he climbs the tree and shakes the branches; figs drop all round him.

L2 ZEB: So it's you.

## S3 Breakfast
beats: B4

Zeb piles figs by the trough.

L3 ZEB: Breakfast, old friend.
