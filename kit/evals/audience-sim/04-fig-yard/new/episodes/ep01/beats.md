# Beats

## B1 introduction
duration: 10

Every morning, figs are missing from the fig yard Zeb looks after.

## B2 development
duration: 10

Zeb hides behind the water trough at dawn to catch the thief.

## B3 twist
duration: 10

The thief is an old tortoise, munching the fallen figs.

## B4 conclusion
duration: 10

Zeb leaves a pile of fallen figs by the trough for the tortoise each morning.
