# Script

## S1 The branch
beats: B1

Fen stands under the oldest tree and counts the apples on the lowest branch.

L1 FEN: Nine, ten, eleven.

## S2 The gate
beats: B2

A goat leans on the orchard gate. Fen looks up from the branch: the goat is still leaning on the gate.

L2 FEN: You again.

## S3 The gap
beats: B3

The gate gives way and the goat is inside.

L3 FEN: Not the low branch.

## S4 The trade
beats: B4

Fen rolls the windfalls to the goat, one by one, and keeps the branch.

L4 FEN: Those are yours. This one is mine.
