format: short-four-act
runtime: 30-45             # seconds, minimum-maximum
beats: introduction, development, twist, conclusion
shots: 4-8
skips: outline
---
Structure: kishotenketsu, one act per shot or pair of shots. It needs no antagonist.

The introduction must establish one character, one place and one ordinary situation. The development
must deepen that situation without changing its direction: the viewer learns more, but nothing is yet
at stake. The twist must bring in an element that seems unrelated and recasts what came before. The
conclusion must reconcile the twist with the introduction so that both read differently together. The
story editor checks that the twist is a surprise of meaning, not a conflict, and that the conclusion
needs both halves.
