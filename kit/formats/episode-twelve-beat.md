Structure: a one-paragraph premise, then twelve beats split three, six and three.

Beats 1 to 3 must set up the character, the situation and the problem, ending on the decision that
starts the episode. Beats 4 to 9 must escalate that problem through attempts that fail or cost
something, each beat a turn and not a repeat of the one before. Beats 10 to 12 must bring the problem
to a climax, resolve it and show the cost or the change in a closing image. Each beat must be one
sentence a reader could draw as one scene. The story editor checks the 3/6/3 split and that every
middle beat changes the situation.
