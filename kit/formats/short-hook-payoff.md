Structure: a hook and its payoff, nothing else.

The hook must land in the first one to three seconds: one image or line that raises a single question
the viewer wants answered. The payoff must answer that exact question, not a different one, and end the
video on the answer rather than on an explanation of it. There is no room for setup, so every character
and place must read at a glance from the first frame. The story editor checks that the payoff could not
be guessed from the hook alone and that nothing on screen serves neither beat.
