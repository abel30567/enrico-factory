Structure: a teaser, three acts and a tag, carrying an A story and a B story.

The teaser must hook with the A story's problem before the title. Act 1 must set up the A story and
the B story and end on a turn that makes the problem worse. Act 2 must complicate both stories and end
on the lowest point of the A story. Act 3 must resolve the A story and let the B story pay off in a way
that comments on it. The tag must give a short closing beat, a joke or an image, that does not reopen
the problem. The story editor checks that each act ends on a turn and that the B story touches the A
story at least once.
