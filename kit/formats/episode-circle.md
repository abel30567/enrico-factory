Structure: the story circle in eight steps, which give eight scenes of two to four shots.

You must establish the character in a zone of comfort; need must show that something is wrong or
missing. Go must cross a threshold into an unfamiliar situation, and search must make the character
adapt to it through trial. Find must give them what they wanted, and take must make them pay a heavy
price for it. Return must bring them back to the familiar situation, and change must show that they
have changed, through action rather than statement. The story editor checks that every step is its own
scene and that the price in take is paid by the character, not by the world.
