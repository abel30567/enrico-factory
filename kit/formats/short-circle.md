format: short-circle
runtime: 45-60             # seconds, minimum-maximum
beats: comfort and want, crossing and search, finding and price, return and change
shots: 4-8
skips: outline
---
Structure: the eight-step story circle folded into four quadrants, either four long shots or eight
short ones.

Comfort and want must show the character at rest and name the one thing they lack. Crossing and search
must move them into unfamiliar ground where they try to get it. Finding and price must give them what
they wanted and make them pay for it in the same beat. Return and change must bring them back to the
opening situation and show, without words where possible, how they are different. The story editor
checks that the ending image answers the opening image.
